About

I am an Assistant Professor of Mechatronics Engineering at Rajamangala University of Technology Rattanakosin, Thailand, where I teach dynamics, control systems, machine learning, and mechatronics. I received the B.S.E. degree in mechanical engineering from the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, in 2014, and the M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in mechanical engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 2017 and 2022.

My current research spans metallic glass micro-manufacturing, cobot-based machine vision, RF and microwave sensing for agricultural products, and engineering education. In micro-manufacturing, I focus on micro-forming and micro-drilling of metallic glasses, analyzing their thermoplastic behavior to produce surgical blades of consistently high quality. I also develop mechatronic systems for healthcare, agriculture, and manufacturing, including robots for industrial automation and computer vision systems for automated agricultural grading.

I work with industrial partners on automated inspection and quality-control platforms, translating laboratory-scale manufacturing insights into tools that enterprises can deploy. I also study collaborative learning and student engagement in engineering design courses.

Reviewer, The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology; IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics; ASME journals.

Research

Precision Micro-Manufacturing

Thermoplastic forming, micro-drilling, and micro-grinding of bulk metallic glasses for surgical blades and cutting tools.

Robotics & Machine Vision

Cobot pick-and-place, 3D point cloud mapping, and deep-learning inspection for manufacturing, healthcare, and agriculture.

RF Sensing & Signal Processing

Nondestructive RF and microwave sensing of agricultural products, and sparse regularization for signal and image denoising.

Engineering Education

Active, project-based, and online learning in design and manufacturing courses.
